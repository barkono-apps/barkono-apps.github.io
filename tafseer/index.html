Tafseer-ul Qur'an Bil Qur'an

Sheikh Yahya Ibrahim Masussuka

The whole numbered Tafseer series of Sheikh Yahya Ibrahim Masussuka on your phone, with the Holy Qur'an in Warsh and Hafs beside it. What you play is kept on your phone, to hear again without data.

Help

A lesson does not play. Check that the phone has a connection; a lesson you played before plays without one. If it still does not play, close the app and open it again.

The sound is too low or too loud. In the player, tap Sound: the Voice, Volume and Quality tabs change how the lessons sound.

The app uses too much space. In Settings, Files cache shows what is kept on the phone and lets you remove it.
