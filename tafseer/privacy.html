Privacy policy

Tafseer-ul Qur'an Bil Qur'an · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Tafseer-ul Qur'an Bil Qur'an mobile app (the lectures of Sheikh Yahya Ibrahim Masussuka) and this website.

What the app keeps on your phone

You do not create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number. The app remembers where you stopped in each lesson, which lessons you finished or saved, and your settings. That information stays on your phone and is deleted when you remove the app.

What our server sees

To show the list of lessons and play audio, the app contacts our server. Like any website, the server and its hosting provider (Cloudflare) receive your IP address and basic technical details of the request, and keep short-lived logs to keep the service running and safe. We do not use them to identify you.

We count how many times each lesson is played, and from which country, so we know what listeners use. These counts are kept by day and hold nothing that identifies you or your phone.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the app first asks whether it may track your activity; if you say no, your advertising ID is not used. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

App settings and notifications

The app uses Google Firebase to receive settings from us and, if you allow notifications, to tell you about new lessons. For this, Firebase creates an anonymous identifier for the app on your phone. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

Clips you share

When you share a clip, the video is made on your phone. It goes only to the app you choose to share it with.

Children

The app is for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
